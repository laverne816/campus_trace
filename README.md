CPUT lost and found for District Six, Bellville, Mowbray, Wellington and Granger Bay.

CAMPUS TRACE is a modern, responsive, accountability-first campus Lost & Found web application designed to help university students safely recover lost belongings and return found property across CPUT campuses, including District Six, Bellville, Mowbray, Wellington, and Granger Bay.

The platform provides a centralised space where students can report lost or found items, browse available listings, search for specific belongings, and reconnect misplaced property with its rightful owners through a simple, accessible, and user-friendly experience.

With a focus on accountability, transparency, and community collaboration, CAMPUS TRACE helps make the recovery process more organised and reliable. Students can submit detailed item reports, including descriptions, categories, locations, and relevant images, making it easier to identify and track belongings. The platform also supports item verification to encourage responsible ownership claims and reduce the risk of belongings being handed over to the wrong person.

Designed with a clean, modern interface and responsive layouts, CAMPUS TRACE provides a consistent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. By bringing lost and found reporting into one accessible digital platform, the application aims to reduce the frustration of losing personal belongings, encourage students to look out for one another, and create a safer, more connected campus community.
